Week Four

Support Activities

Use your Support Activity time to connect with students and follow through on their needs.

When to Work on Support Activities

Support Activities are a required part of your internship. Set aside time for them according to the calendaring requirements. Also use time set aside for appointments or live events when no students have booked or signed up.

These activities let you support students outside scheduled sessions while building your coaching skills and contributing to the team. Find instructions, documents, and templates on the Support Activities page in SharePoint.

Update Your Calendar

After you complete the activity, right-click the Support Activity block in Outlook and choose This event to edit that occurrence only. Rename the block to reflect the activity you completed.

Log Student Interactions

Use the Interaction Data Form to log student interactions from these Support Activities:

  • Community Exchanges. Log posts and replies by course.
  • Student Touchpoints. Log a completed back-and-forth as an Email Exchange.
  • Outreach Calling. Log outreach calls and follow-up emails under their corresponding categories.

Appointment Training

Peer Navigator Appointments

Prepare for an appointment, place a call in Genesys, and follow the steps for each call outcome.

Signing into Genesys for the First Time

  • Select More Login Options.
  • Enter westerngovernorsuniversity as your organization and click next
  • Sign in using your PingID SSO.

Before the Appointment

  • Send the Peer Navigator Appointment Confirmation email.
  • Review the appointment details on your Outlook calendar.
  • Look up the student in the Call Verification Dashboard and keep it open before placing the call.
  • Use your WGU-issued Jabra headset during appointments. This is required.
  • Open and lock your personal Webex room so the next student can’t join the current appointment early.

Tip. Use the dial pad to test your audio.

Placing a Call in Genesys

  1. In the left menu, click Workspace, then Agent Workspace.
  2. Find the arrow pointing toward the mail icon next to the left menu. Click the phone icon to the left of the mail icon to Start a new call.
  3. Select your school’s queue to call on its behalf.
  4. Enter the student’s phone number and press the green call button.

Important. Always call through your school’s queue so Genesys records the call.

Skip the Go on Queue button and the call button on the right side of the screen. Those controls skip the queue selection prompt.

Call Outcomes

Student Doesn’t Answer

Missed Call/No Student Process

  1. Leave a FERPA-compliant voicemail letting the student know you’ll call back in 5 minutes.
    “Hi ___! This is ___ from WGU. I will send an email to your account and call back in 5 minutes. Hope to hear from you soon!”
  2. Send the WGU Peer Navigator - Just Called email.
  3. Wait 5 minutes and call back. If the student answers, follow the appointment process.
  4. If the student misses the second call, leave another FERPA-compliant voicemail and send the WGU Peer Navigator - Missed Appointment email.
    “Hi ___! This is ___ from WGU. I will send an email to your account. I hope we can connect another time!”
  5. Document the attempt in the Student Note Form and categorize the appointment as No Student (red) on your calendar.

Student Answers

Appointment Process

  1. Greet the student and verify their identity using three points of identification.
    • First and last name
    • Program Mentor’s name. If the student has no assigned Program Mentor, use their Enrollment Counselor’s name.
    • Student ID. If it’s unavailable, you can accept their username.
  2. Work through the AFFIRM model based on the student’s needs.
  3. Share your screen in Webex on every call except accountability calls.
  4. On every call, facilitate strategy practice, demonstrate how to access resources, or do both. Use only strategies and resources from the Peer Navigator Toolbox or approved course or WGU resources.
  5. If you have the next time slot free, you may offer to extend the appointment. If the student agrees, pause briefly and use the scheduling link to book them into the next available slot.
  6. At the end of the call, encourage the student to schedule a follow-up while they’re still on the line. Ask them to complete the survey in the post-appointment email.

After the Appointment

  1. Complete and submit the Student Note Form on the same day as the appointment. Follow all note etiquette guidelines.
    • Use course code ORA1 for new students in Orientation.
    • Use N/A only when absolutely necessary.
  2. Complete and send the Post-Appointment Summary email.
  3. Categorize the appointment as Coaching (gold) on your calendar.

Interaction Data reminder. Exclude routine emails, such as pre- and post-appointment emails, from email exchanges in the Interaction Data log.

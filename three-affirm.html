AFFIRM Framework

What is AFFIRM?

AFFIRM is WGU's very own coaching framework for academic success. A framework helps guide your coaching sessions by providing a clear structure and sequence. Instead of wondering what to do next, the framework gives you a roadmap that keeps both you and the student focused, intentional, and moving toward their learning goals. It helps ensure consistency in your approach and creates a supportive environment where students feel guided and understood.

The AFFIRM framework has six interconnected stages, each building on the last. Think of it as a fluid process—you may find yourself moving back and forth between stages as you clarify goals, re-explain strategies, or deepen understanding. The framework adapts to the student's needs.

Start by understanding what the student wants to accomplish. Help them articulate their goals and reflect on their current progress and learning experiences.

Help the student clearly articulate and understand their specific challenges. Work together to define the problem so you can move forward with targeted support.

Introduce and discuss targeted strategies or approaches. Demonstrate one strategy in action and explore alternatives together. Work collaboratively to build a game plan.

Support the student as they actively practice the strategy. Provide constructive feedback and help them work through challenges in real time.

Help the student synthesize their learning and identify actionable next steps. Explore how this strategy builds their confidence and independence.

Encourage the student to identify their path ahead and celebrate their progress. Foster motivation and accountability for continued growth.

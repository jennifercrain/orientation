Welcome to WGU!

"Changing Lives for the Better by Creating Pathways to Opportunity"

Welcome!

Welcome to this exciting journey of becoming an intern offering peer support at WGU! We're thrilled to have you join us. Over the next few weeks, you'll gain the knowledge and skills to empower others, foster growth, and make a positive impact.

Through this program, you will develop the skills to cultivate meaningful peer relationships, utilize powerful coaching strategies, and play a vital role in your peers' academic achievements. We can't wait to see you flourish as a skilled and compassionate peer!

This hands-on training encourages active participation. Stay focused, limit distractions, and reflect on the material to maximize your learning. By holding yourself accountable, you'll not only support your peers but also enhance your own understanding. Please be fully present during training.

Training Expectations

Training Completion

You must complete all training in the provided order and attend all scheduled workshops. Do not skip ahead. Remember to complete and submit all checkpoints to ensure your progress is recorded.

Sequential Training

Upon completion of the week's training, do not move forward to the next week. This sequential design allows for a focused understanding of specific aspects of your role. Please contact your Supervisor if you complete training early.

Quizzes and Progress

You will encounter quizzes throughout the course. Each quiz has limited attempts and, sometimes, a cooldown period before it can be taken again. Your progress and responses will be reviewed by your leaders each week.

Peer Support Models

WGU offers two types of main support services, each with its own focus and approach.

Appointments

Peer-to-peer appointments center around helping students to problem-solve, set goals, and improve their overall performance and success at WGU in a one-on-one setting. Peer Navigators use various strategies that encourage students to think critically and self-select solutions that work best for them.

Live Events

Peer-led live events are centered around learning strategies and helping students develop better study skills. Peer Navigators use various strategies that encourage students to engage with the course material and their fellow peers.

Getting Started

You will need to complete all checkpoints to get credit for completion. Please submit all quizzes and select "Mark as Done" at the bottom of the page.

If you encounter technical errors with the course (such as broken links), please reach out to Jennifer Crain on Teams.

Week 2

Coaching Observations

Observe appointments and live events to develop your coaching skills!

You’ll sign up to observe a minimum of two appointments and/or live events, depending on your role. Plan ahead! Each intern on your team was instructed to share their calendar with you when you began training. Live event schedules are available on the Live Events page of our team SharePoint. If you cannot access the calendars or schedules, please reach out to your Supervisor.

If you’re observing appointments, reach out to the intern on Teams. They will invite you to a Teams meeting where they share their audio and screen during their appointment. If you’re observing live events, add your name to the observation column on your course schedule. You will join the live event (listed in Mountain Time) using the course’s Webex button on the Live Events page.

Set up your observations, complete the Observation Form below, then progress through training while you wait for your observation time.

Observation Setup

Appointment Observations

If your role includes appointment observations, find available appointments on your team’s calendars and message the intern on Teams to ask to observe their call.

Setup Steps:

  1. 1. Find appointments on team calendars
  2. 2. Message the intern to ask permission
  3. 3. Click appointment to see course & focus area
  4. 4. Listen intently and complete observation form
  5. 5. Consider what you would do differently

Calendar Naming:

[Your Name] 1:1 Observation [Intern Name]

Example: Jarrad Markel 1:1 Observation Shelby Hulland

Toggle "Teams meeting" ON for 1:1 observations

Note: Camera and microphone not required. Your honest reflections are strictly between you and your Supervisor.

Live Event Observations

If your role includes live event observations, sign up early for the 60-minute sessions you plan to observe over the next week. Most courses run sessions Sunday through Wednesday.

Setup Steps:

  1. 1. Review Discussion Map Template
  2. 2. Check the Live Events page on our team SharePoint for schedules
  3. 3. Add initials to PC Shadow column
  4. 4. Copy Webex link to calendar event
  5. 5. Toggle "Teams meeting" OFF

Calendar Naming:

[Course Code] [Session Title] Observation ([Intern Name])

Example: D082 Making the Most of Your Time Observation (Shelby)

Note: Camera and microphone not required. Your honest reflections are strictly between you and your Supervisor.

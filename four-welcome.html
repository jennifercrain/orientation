Week Four

Welcome to Week Four

Put your coaching skills into practice and prepare to support students across the Peer Navigator role.

Overview

This week, you’ll bring together what you’ve learned in a course material mock session. You’ll also learn how to support students outside appointments and live events and practice using Success Tools to help students take a manageable next step.

Set aside focused time to complete each page and activity in this module.

Why This Matters

Support students during and between sessions

The mock session gives you a chance to practice coaching with the course material you’ll use in your role. Support Activities give you ways to stay connected with students between sessions, including following up by email and engaging in course communities. Success Tools help you work with students on barriers such as finding time to study, getting started, or recovering after a setback.

How This Week Works

Complete Your Course Material Mock Session

Prepare for your assigned topic and practice using course material and coaching strategies in the appointment or live event format assigned to you.

Learn How to Provide Direct Student Support

Explore the Support Activities you’ll complete outside appointments and live events. Learn how to follow up through Email Exchanges and support students through Community Support in WGU Connect.

Practice Using Success Tools

Explore burnout and motivation, goal setting, mindset, resilience, and time management. Practice asking questions that help students identify what’s getting in the way and decide how they’ll apply a strategy.

What Success Looks Like This Week

By the end of this week, you can

  • Use course material and coaching strategies in your assigned mock session.
  • Explain how Email Exchanges and Community Support fit into your Support Activity time.
  • Choose a Success Tool based on what the student shares and help them identify how they’ll use it.

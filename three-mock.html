Week Three

Course-Based Mock Session

Next in your training, you’ll complete a course-based mock session. You’ll practice supporting a student using material from your assigned course. A Senior Peer will play the role of the student, and the Training Supervisor or your leader will observe.

Find your mock and confirm your topic

Appointment mock

Look for a 30-minute meeting on your calendar that mimics a student appointment. The meeting title will include the Senior Peer’s first and last name and student ID. Open the meeting details to find the course and topic you’ll prepare for.

Live Event mock

Look for a 30–60-minute meeting on your calendar. The length depends on your school. For your mock, you’ll use the first topic you’re scheduled to facilitate live. You can find it on your course’s Live Event schedule.

Review your topic and choose two strategies

As you review the course material, consider where a student might get stuck.

  • How is the material presented? Is the text dense, wordy, or hard to follow?
  • What makes the topic challenging? Does it include several subtopics? Are any concepts hard to visualize or easy to confuse?

Choose two strategies that could help a student work through the challenges you identified.

Practice modeling your strategies

Prepare for the “I do” portion of Focused Instruction. Practice how you’ll:

  • Share your screen. Display the course material and strategy template side by side. Zoom in so both are easy to read, and keep the part of the template you’re working on in view.
  • Introduce the strategy. Briefly explain what it is, how it works, why it can help with this topic, and when a student might use it.
  • Choose an example. Decide where you’ll start and stop so the student can see how the strategy works with the course material.
  • Think aloud as you model. Practice saying what you notice, what you decide to do next, and why. Let the student hear the reasoning behind each step.

Complete your mock and meet with your leader

During the mock, your observer will stay off camera and watch for coaching strengths and opportunities for growth. After the mock, you’ll meet with your leader to discuss what went well and what you can work on.

Reflect on your mock

After meeting with your leader, complete the activity for your role.

  • Appointment interns complete an example Student Note Form.
  • Live Event interns complete a mock session reflection on their Discussion Map.

Then, watch your mock session recording and complete the self-evaluation activity on the Support Activities page. Use it to identify the coaching skills you want to work on.

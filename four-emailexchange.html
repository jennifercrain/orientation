Email Exchanges

Use your Support Activity block to email students from previous appointments or live events and offer support and encouragement.

Use or adapt the email templates when writing your follow-up.

Calendaring

Right-click and update your Support Activity block in Outlook. Select This event to edit only that occurrence after you have completed the activity. Then, rename it Support Activity - Student Touchpoints.

Who to Contact

Appointment Interns

  • Students you met with in a previous appointment, ideally two weeks ago or more.
  • Students who missed or canceled an appointment.

Where to look. Review your Outlook calendar for students who previously scheduled with you or whose appointments you marked as missed.

Live Event Interns

  • Students who recently attended your live events. Use the Webex attendance report to identify them.
  • Students who registered for your live events and did not attend.

Where to look. On the Support Activity page, expand the section for your school. The Excel lists show students who registered for your session and did not attend, as well as students who attended sessions the previous week. The Events Supervisor updates the lists every Monday.

When to Log an Email Exchange

Log an Email Exchange once both you and the student have participated. If you send multiple emails before the student replies, count the completed exchange once.

Interaction Data

On the Interaction Data Form, enter 1 for each completed Email Exchange.

  • You initiate the exchange. You email the student and they reply. Log 1.
  • The student initiates the exchange. The student emails you and you reply. Log 1.

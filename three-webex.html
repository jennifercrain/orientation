Week Three

Webex Functions & Features

Practice sharing your screen, popping out panels, docking windows, and switching between them until you can do each comfortably.

Webex Setup

Download the Webex application (required)

Screen sharing is required for Peer Navigators.

For appointments, keep your personal room locked so the next student can’t join an appointment in progress. Live event rooms must remain unlocked at all times.

Screensharing in Webex

  1. At the bottom of Webex, click Share.
  2. Select the screen or application you want students to see.
  3. Uncheck Show my Webex app to keep Webex panels, such as Participants, out of the shared view.
  4. Click Share.

If you’re sharing a video, check Include computer sound in the right-hand menu before you share.

Cameras, Chat, and Participants

  1. When you start sharing, your camera view should pop out automatically.
  2. In the camera view, click the person icon to open Participants.
  3. In the camera view, click the three dots for more options and select Chat.

The video may show an earlier version of Webex. Use the written steps for this training.

Docking Windows

  1. Open two separate windows and select the one you want on the left.
  2. Hold the Windows key and press the Left Arrow to dock the selected window on the left.
  3. Release the Windows key. When prompted, select the window you want on the right.
  4. To resize both windows, drag the divider between them.

Methods for Switching Between Windows

Method 1: Alt+Tab

  1. Hold Alt and press Tab to display your open windows.
  2. Keep holding Alt and tap Tab to cycle through the windows.
  3. Release Alt when the window you want is highlighted.

Method 2: Tab Dragging

  1. With your windows docked, open the tabs you’ll need in a browser window on your other screen.
  2. Click and drag the tab you need into the browser window on the screen you’re sharing.

Annotate Tool

  1. After you start sharing, look for the orange sharing bar at the top of the shared screen.
  2. Next to You’re sharing, click the square icon with a plus sign to open More sharing options.
  3. Select Annotate to open the toolbar. Use it to circle, highlight, or add text directly on the shared screen or application.

The video may show an earlier version of Webex. Use the written steps for this training.

Annotation Tools

  • Laser pointer draws attention to an area with a mark that fades after a few seconds.
  • Arrow pointer stamps an arrow with your name. Click another area to move it, or use the paintbrush icon (fourth from the bottom) to remove arrow stamps.
  • Pen draws marks that stay on the screen until you erase or clear them.
  • Text adds a typed message.
  • Eraser removes pen or text markings.
  • Undo/Redo reverses or restores the last action.
  • Clear all removes all pointers and annotations.

Tip. To give participants annotation privileges, select the gear icon on the Annotate toolbar and choose Allow anyone to annotate.

Webex Whiteboards

  1. After you start sharing, use the three dots in the pop-out camera view to open more options, then select Whiteboards.
  2. Select New whiteboard, then Open for all.
  3. If Webex asks you to stop sharing your current screen or application, click Continue.
  4. Use the tools on the left to add pen marks, shapes, sticky notes, text, or emojis.
  5. Select Close for all in the upper right to end the whiteboard. If you were sharing before opening it, select Resume on the gray sharing bar at the top of your screen to return to your previous share.

Slido Polling

  1. After you start sharing, use the three dots in the pop-out camera view and select More panels.
  2. Select Slido (polling and Q&A), or open Apps and search for Slido.
  3. Click Create new slido, then + Add.
  4. Choose an interaction type: Multiple choice, Word cloud, Open text, Ranking, Rating, Quiz, or Survey.
  5. Follow the on-screen prompts, then click the green Launch button to start the poll.
  6. Click the red stop button to end the poll.
